How this radio works

It plays internet radio stations and checks, in your browser, which ones are actually on air.

◉ iPod look
A black click-wheel player that fills the screen (also under Settings → Look). Turn the wheel to move the highlight, press the centre to pick, MENU goes back (hold it for the main menu), ⏮ ⏭ change station, ▶❚❚ plays or pauses. On Now Playing the wheel is the volume. Open a folding phone, or use a tablet or computer, and the station guide sits on the left page with the iPod on the right.
On a small phone
The radio becomes a 1950s push-button car radio. Tap a name on the dial or swipe it to tune, use the five keys as presets (tap an empty key to store the station that’s on, hold a key to replace it), and press STATIONS for search and filters.
Station guide
Tap a station to play it. Rows turn silver once a station is on air and brown when it’s off air.
Search & chips
Filter the list you have, search all your lists, or search all of Radio Browser (about 50,000 stations). The chips (Near me, Jazz, News…) browse Radio Browser; save what you find as a new list.
Lists
Pick, add (+), reload (⟳) or remove (🗑) a list at the top of the station guide. On the shortwave radio the lists are also the BAND knob: FM is the first, MW the second, SW1–SW8 the next eight. On the iPod, tap the title bar to filter the stations as you type.
TUNING
Moves through the stations. Tap the left or right half of the knob, scroll on it, or drag it round. It tunes in when you stop.
Under the dial
⏮ ⏭ previous / next station · ⏸ pause (Space) · ⏹ stop · 🔊 mute (M) · ● LIVE jumps back to the live broadcast after a pause.
POWER
Play / stop. If a stream drops, the radio reconnects by itself.
SENS DX / LOCAL
DX shows every station; LOCAL hides the ones that are off air or blocked.
⏾ Sleep
Next to the play controls: pick 30 minutes to 3 hours, in 30-minute steps. It counts down there, then fades the sound out and turns the radio off.
MEMORY · LIGHT
Save a favorite · dial lamp and effects.
♪ Songs
The song playing shows on the dial, the car LCD, the phone bar and the iPod; tap it for the song card with YouTube, Spotify and Apple Music links and what played before it. ♪ Songs in the header lists everything you heard.
