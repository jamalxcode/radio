Only stations that passed Radio Browser’s own check are loaded. This app then tests them again in your browser.

Settings

Your data

Lists, favorites, ratings, songs heard and test results are saved only in this browser. Use a backup to move them to another browser or device.

Songs you heard

Titles the stations sent while you listened, newest first. Saved only in this browser.

How this radio works

It plays internet radio stations and checks, in your browser, which ones are actually on air.

Station guide
Tap a station to play it. Rows turn silver once a station is on air and brown when it’s off air.
Search & chips
Filter the list you have, search all your lists, or search all of Radio Browser (about 50,000 stations). The chips (Near me, Jazz, News…) browse Radio Browser; save what you find as a new list.
BAND
Your station lists: FM is the first, MW the second, SW1–SW8 the next eight. Tap a band on the dial or turn the knob.
TUNING
Moves through the stations. Tap the left or right half of the knob, scroll on it, or drag it round. It tunes in when you stop.
Under the dial
⏮ ⏭ previous / next station · ⏸ pause (Space) · ⏹ stop · 🔊 mute (M) · ● LIVE jumps back to the live broadcast after a pause.
POWER
Play / stop. If a stream drops, the radio reconnects by itself.
SENS DX / LOCAL
DX shows every station; LOCAL hides the ones that are off air or blocked.
⏾ Sleep
Next to the play controls: pick 5 minutes to 3 hours. It counts down there, then fades the sound out and turns the radio off.
MEMORY · LIGHT
Save a favorite · dial lamp and effects.
♪ Songs
Song and show titles you heard, with links to find them on YouTube, Spotify or Apple Music.
